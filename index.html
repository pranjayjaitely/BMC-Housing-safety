BMC Building Safety

Help

How do I check my building's status?

Search by address, PIN code or society name from the home screen.

What do C1, C2A, C2B and C3 mean?

C1: dangerous. C2A: partly unsafe. C2B: major repairs. C3: minor repairs.

Who maintains this data?

Records are taken from published BMC lists. Check the source date shown with each result.
