BMC Building Safety

Help

How do I check my building's status?

Search by address, PIN code or society name from the home screen, or find your building on the map.

What do C1, C2A, C2B and C3 mean?

C1: dangerous, to be vacated and demolished. C2A: partly unsafe, repairs after vacating the dangerous part. C2B: major repairs, residents can stay. C3: minor repairs only.

Who maintains this data?

Records come from the BMC Building and Factories Department's Technical Advisory Committee, refreshed on a regular cycle.
